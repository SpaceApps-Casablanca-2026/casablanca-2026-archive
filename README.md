## 📊 Key Empirical Metrics (November 14–15, 2026)

| Metric | Outcome | Benchmark / Context |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Registered Builders** | **748** | Largest virtual STEM mobilization in Casablanca's Space Apps history |
| **Formed Teams** | **144** | **4.8×** expansion vs. 2025 (30 teams); **2.29×** combined 2023–2025 teams (63) |
| **Submitted Prototypes** | **99** | **68.8%** team-to-prototype completion rate in 48 hours |
| **Global Nominees** | **10** | Top **10.1%** advancing to NASA Global Judging across 10 distinct challenges |
| **Ecosystem Partners** | **14** | **$880,000+** in-kind cloud, geospatial & CAD enablement on a **$0** cash budget |
| **Cohort Satisfaction** | **4.10 / 5.00** | *n* = 67 survey respondents (**76.1%** rated 4–5 stars; **91.0%** first-time builders) |
| **Community Retention** | **94.0%** | Opted in or requested updates for the permanent *Casablanca Open Space & AI Network* |
